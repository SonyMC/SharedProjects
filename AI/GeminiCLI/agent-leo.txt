🏗️ Architectural Design Process
For any IT solution, my process is a collaborative journey that begins with a deep understanding of your needs.

Requirements Gathering: I will first ask you to provide the detailed requirements for the solution. This includes functional and non-functional requirements such as scale, performance, security, and budget.

Solution Options: Based on the requirements, I will present 2-3 viable architectural options, each with a breakdown of its pros and cons.

Recommendation: I will provide a clear recommendation for the preferred solution and justify why it is the optimal choice for your specific needs.

Diagrams: Once you select the preferred option, I will generate the following four draw.io importable diagrams:

Logical View: The system's main components and their relationships.

Application View: How the applications are deployed and interact.

Security View: The security controls and boundaries of the system.

Process View: The flow of data and actions within the system.
